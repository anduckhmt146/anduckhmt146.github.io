Here is solutions for Social Script Vault.

## 1. Work & Professional

### Ask for a raise 

- What you do in timeframe

- What impact the team

- Based on current market rates -> promotion

### Decline additional work

- Full capacity of project

- Ensure I deliver quality work on my existing commitment.

- Will revisit when my schedule more flexibility.

### Address a mistake

- I have a mistake is [specific error]

- I understand the impact is [specific consequences]

- Action to correct it

- Future prevention

### Follow-up unanswered email

- I follow-up for email from [date] about [subject]

- Deadline

- Do you need any information from me to move forward, let have a discussion or meeting ?

- or please check it

### Request feedback

- Appreciate your feedback

- Understand what work well + what to improve

- Help me devlop skills for better results

### Set boundaries with colleagues

- I notice I am be interrupt -> make me can not focus.

- Could we agree that in 2-4pm i will be unavailable.

- I happy to help other time.

### Dealing with credit-stealing coworker

- I notice my idea is presented without acknowledge my contribution.

- It's important for me that my work is recognized appropriately

- In the future, I'd appreciate proper attribution when my work is shared. How can we ensure this happens going forward.

### Networking follow-up

- It was great to meet you at [event] yesterday and discuss about [topic]

- I particularly enjoyed your insights about [point]

- I would love to [grab a coffee, add friend] so that discuss more about it.

- Would you be available next week ?

### Request letter for recommendation

- I am applying [program] and gather recommendation letters to speak about [experience]

- Given our work together about [skill], I believe you could provide valuable insight about abilities.

- Would you willing to write a letter of my recommendation

- I'd happy to provide any information that make this easier to you, including a draft of points to consider.

- The deadline is [date]

### Resigning from a position

- Hi manager, I've made the decision to resign for [role]

- My last day will be [date]

- I appreciate the opportunies to grow and learn here over the past [time].

- I'd like a smooth transition, please help me with knowledege transfer + training new member during my notice period

### Adress workplace conflict

- Hi [name], I've noticed some tension between us regarding [topic]

- I would like to discuss it with you.

- My expectation is find a solution that work for both us and improve relationship.

- Could we set aside some time to talk through this privately ? 

- I value of perspective and belive we can resolve this constructively

### Request flexible work arrangements

- I'd like to discuss the posibility of [specific arrangement, e.g. working remotely 2 day per week]

- That would help me 'reduce my commute time' and 'better manage family responsibilities' -> while maintaining my productivity.

- I've thought about how to ensure it do not impact my work quality and team collaboration.

- I'd happy to discuss my plan for making this successful.

### Response to criticism

- Thank you for brining my attention. I appreciate your feedback abot [topic]

- I understandd the [impact of the issue] and take responsibility for that.

- Action to improve

- Catch up it again at [timeframe] to review my progress.

### Ask for help with workload

- I have manage serveral projects including [a,b,c]

- I concern about delivering everything at the quality level expected.

- Could we discuss options for redistributing tasks for others or adjusting timeline.

- I have identified some [specific tasks] that might be good candidates for reassignment and deadline extension

### Decline a work social event

- Thank you for invitation to [event]

- Unfortunately, I won't be able to attend due to prior commitment.

- I appreciate being included and looking forward to next team event when my schedule allows.

## 2. Dating & Relationship

### Ask someone out

- I really enjoy to know you over [context]

- I would like to continue our conversation in [coffee/dinner/activity]

- Would you be interested in joining me at [date]

### Turn down a date

- Thank you for invitation

- I don't feel the same connection

- I value [relationship] and hope we can continue to [friend]

### End things after a few dates

- I've enjoyed getting you know over these past few weeks.

- You are [compliment], but I don't feel the connection

- I want to be honest rather than just fade away. I wish you all the best.

### Break up for long-term relationship

- I've been thinking a lot about our relationship, and I feel we should end our relationship.

- I care you deeply, which why I want to be honest with [appropriate reason]

- I know this is painful, I'm truly sorry.

- I hope we can handle this transition with mutual respect.

### Setting physical boundaries

- I would like to have conversation about physical relationship.

- I very much enjoy [specific activities you comfortable with] and appreciate the connection we're building.

- At this point, I want to maintain the boundary and not progress to [activity over the comfort level] until I feel ready.

- I value our relationship and want it feel right for both of us.

- I welcome your thoughts and feelings on this matter, as your comfort is equally important to me.

### Define the relationship talk

- I've been enjoying the time we've spending together and self-thinking about you often.

- I'd like to talk about where we stand and what we're both looking for.

- I'm interest in [desired relationship] e.g. exclusive relationship and continue to date but keeping thing casual.

- How have you been feeling about us ?

### Address relationship concerns

- When [specific situation/behaviour occur], I feel it [affect] you.

- I value our relationship and want us to work through this together.

- Could we discuss how we might address this.
